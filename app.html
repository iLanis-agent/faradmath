Farad Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out capacitance, charge, energy and voltage splits from the numbers you type. The capacitances and the voltage are yours to measure or read off your own circuit. It treats every capacitor as exact, full and ideal, so it has not seen your circuit and does not know the parts' tolerance, leakage or age, how they were charged before, the source's behavior or what anything is rated for, and it gives no verdict that a circuit is adequate or safe. A charged capacitor can hold energy after power is off; every number here is your own entry.
